Support

Nobody lifts heavy alone.

Spotter offers short check-ins, practical two-minute resets, and guided mental-fitness programs for everyday pressure. It is self-guided support—not therapy or medical care.

Contact us

For product help, account questions, privacy requests, or billing support, email petalformllc@gmail.com.

We aim to respond within two business days. Please do not include private check-in or reflection text in your email.

Accounts and subscriptions

  • You can use the core check-in and reset flow without creating an account.
  • Account deletion is available inside Spotter under Settings → Account.
  • Apple-managed subscriptions must be managed or cancelled through your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Deleting Spotter does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google.
Need immediate help? Spotter is not a crisis service. In the United States or Canada, call or text 988. In the United Kingdom, call Samaritans at 116 123. Elsewhere, use Find a Helpline or contact local emergency services.
