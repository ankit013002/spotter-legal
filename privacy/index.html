Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

Spotter is designed around a simple boundary: what you write about your inner life should not become our content or an advertising profile.

The short version: your mood, energy, focus, private notes, reset reflections, and streak history stay on your device. They are not uploaded to Spotter’s servers, analytics, or crash reports.

Who we are

Spotter is operated by PETALFORM LLC (“Spotter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). Questions or privacy requests can be sent to petalformllc@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your device

The core mental-fitness experience stores the following locally on your device:

  • mood and energy check-ins;
  • the focus you select, such as pressure or confidence;
  • optional private notes and reset reflections;
  • check-in history, streaks, program progress, and reminder preferences.

We do not receive this content. If you delete the app without a device backup, it may not be recoverable. Signing out or switching accounts clears registered private local stores to help prevent one person’s reflections from appearing for another person on a shared device.

Information we process when you use account features

You can use the core flow as a guest. If you create an account or use optional connected services, we may process:

  • Account information: email address, an internal user identifier, authentication records, and account timestamps.
  • Subscription information: product, entitlement, renewal, expiration, and transaction identifiers from Apple, Google, Stripe, or RevenueCat. We do not receive complete payment-card details from store purchases.
  • Notification information: a device push token and category preferences if you opt into remote notifications while signed in.
  • Support communications: information you choose to send when contacting us.

Analytics and diagnostics

When enabled in a production build, Spotter may collect limited product events and diagnostics such as app version, device platform, screen performance, program starts or completions, subscription events, and technical error details. These systems are configured to exclude private check-ins, mood, energy, selected focus, notes, reflections, email addresses, names, and message content.

Native analytics may use an internal account identifier after sign-in. Web analytics run only after the visitor opts in. Crash reporting may use an internal account identifier to connect repeated technical errors, but not your private reflection content.

How we use information

We use the limited server-side information described above to authenticate accounts, deliver requested notifications, provide and reconcile subscriptions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, improve reliability, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use private reflection content for advertising.

Service providers

Depending on the platform and features you choose, information may be processed by Supabase for authentication and account records; Expo for application delivery and push notifications; Apple or Google for store distribution and purchases; RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management; Stripe for web billing; Sentry for diagnostics; and PostHog for limited product analytics. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

Local check-in data remains on your device until you remove it, sign out where applicable, or delete the app. Account and service records are retained while your account is active and for the limited additional period needed for security, transaction records, dispute handling, legal obligations, and backups.

You can request account deletion inside Spotter. Deletion removes the authentication account and associated server profile and clears Spotter’s registered local private stores. Store providers may retain purchase history required for accounting, fraud prevention, or restoring purchases. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a store-managed subscription.

Your choices and rights

You can use the core app as a guest, decline notifications, decline web analytics, manage store subscriptions through your platform account, and delete your Spotter account in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information. Contact us to exercise those rights.

Children

Spotter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child has provided account information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Safety and sensitive information

Spotter is a self-guided mental-fitness tool, not therapy, diagnosis, or medical care. Do not send private reflection text or urgent crisis information in a support email. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.

Changes

We may update this policy as Spotter changes. We will revise the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material changes apply prospectively.
